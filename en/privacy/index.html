Privacy Policy

Effective: August 28, 2026

SwatchPocket (the “App”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information we collect

The App may store information you create—such as color palettes, colors, names, and settings—on your device. Advertising or analytics providers may also collect device information, App usage data, advertising identifiers, and crash information to improve service quality and deliver advertising.

2. How information is used

  • To provide App features and retain your settings
  • To investigate issues and improve quality and usability
  • To display and measure ads and prevent fraud
  • To respond to support requests

3. Advertising

The App may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob. These providers may use information such as advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not share personally identifiable information with third parties without your consent, except when required by law. Service providers described in this policy may process information only as necessary to perform their services.

5. Data management and deletion

You can delete data stored on your device using the App’s deletion features or by uninstalling the App. To request deletion of information submitted through a support inquiry, please contact us through the support page.

6. Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent where required by applicable law.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in law or the App. Material changes will be announced on this page or within the App.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, please visit our support page.
