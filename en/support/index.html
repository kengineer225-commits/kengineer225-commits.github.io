Frequently asked questions

Where are my saved colors stored?

Your colors and palettes are generally stored on your device. Uninstalling the App may also remove this data.

The App is not working as expected.

Close and reopen the App, and make sure you have the latest version. If the issue continues, tell us your device model, OS version, and the steps that caused it.

How can I delete my data?

Use the deletion features within the App. You can also remove all locally stored App data by uninstalling the App.

Can I suggest a feature or report a bug?

Yes. Please use the contact link below and include as much helpful detail as possible.

Contact us

Bug reports, feature requests, and other questions are accepted through GitHub Issues. Please do not include personal or confidential information.
