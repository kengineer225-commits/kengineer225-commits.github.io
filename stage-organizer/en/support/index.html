Frequently asked questions

Where is my production data stored?

Core production data is stored on your iPhone. There is no account registration or synchronization with a server operated by the Developer. Uninstalling the App may remove your data.

How do I share documents?

Generate a PDF or ZIP in the App, then choose a destination or recipient in the iOS share sheet. Check the content and destination before sending.

I purchased “Remove Ads,” but still see ads.

Confirm that you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for purchase, then try the restore purchases option in the App. If the issue continues, contact us with your device, iOS version, and App version—without personal production data.

How can I delete my data?

Delete the relevant production in the App. You can remove all locally stored App data by uninstalling the App. PDFs or ZIPs saved elsewhere must be deleted at their destination.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email us with your device model, iOS and App versions, and steps to reproduce the issue. Please do not attach confidential production material or personal information.
